Logo and color spec for Greenways, the app that walks Texas Greener Pastures buyers through a property in 3D before they drive out to see it.

Version 1.0, September 2026

The mark

A trail from your feet to the horizon
The cream path starts wide at the bottom edge and narrows as it winds toward the hills, the same walk-in view a 3D tour gives a buyer.
The Lone Star as the destination
Harvest Gold, carried over from TGP, marks where the path is headed: the buyer's own piece of Texas.
Contour lines in the field
Faint topographic lines nod to the survey and terrain data behind each tour. They drop out of the app icon and one-color versions, where they would blur.
A badge, like the parent brand
The ringed circle and slab-serif wordmark keep Greenways in the TGP family. "by Texas Greener Pastures" stays attached wherever it can be read.

Logo files

Every version ships as SVG (the master) with PNG exports. The wordmark is converted to outlines, so the files render the same on any machine without fonts installed.

Clear space and minimum size

Keep a clear margin of X on every side, where X is one quarter of the emblem's diameter. Nothing else (text, photo edges, other logos) enters that space.

Two families, both free on Google Fonts under the Open Font License.

Interface and body text, 400, 500 and 700. Built for low-vision readers, so lot numbers, acreage and payment figures stay unmistakable on a phone in bright sun.

Don't

  • Recolor the emblem outside the full-color and two one-color versions supplied.
  • Retype "Greenways" in live text as the logo. Use the outlined SVG.
  • Set Harvest Gold or Trailhead Green text on Prairie Cream. Use Gold Ink or Trailhead Deep.
  • Place the full-color emblem straight onto busy aerial or drone photos. Put it on a Pine Shadow panel or use the cream one-color version.
  • Stretch, rotate, outline or add drop shadows to any version.
  • Use Fence Post Red for anything but errors and sold lots.

Before production

  • Run a USPTO trademark search on "Greenways" for software and real estate classes before printing signage. The word is common in trail and parks naming.
  • Confirm the shared greens and gold against TGP's original logo source file if one exists; these values came from TGP's existing digital assets, not an official style guide.
  • Match Pantone references from a physical swatch book for gate signs and apparel. None are specified here because on-screen matching is unreliable.
  • Higgsfield renders are presentation and mockup images. The SVG files are the production masters.
